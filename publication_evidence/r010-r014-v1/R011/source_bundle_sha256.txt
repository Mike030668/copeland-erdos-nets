STATUS: PARTIAL / NOT FULLY PERSISTED AT EXECUTION

The seed-atomic supervisor staged src/ + scripts/ + configs/ + vm daemon into
.tmp/ce_pkg_seed.tar.gz and computed a bundle SHA-256 per fire (BSHA), passed to
the VM as CE_BUNDLE_SHA256; the VM daemon recomputed and HARD-STOPPED on mismatch
(so every accepted block ran a bundle whose VM-side sha == launch-side sha).

However, the per-seed package did NOT persist source_provenance.json (that field
existed only in the earlier confirmation daemon, not the seed daemon). Therefore:
- VM-side bundle-sha verification DID occur at runtime (via HARD-STOP gate),
- but the exact bytes/sha are NOT recoverable from the frozen package.

This gap is documented, not reconstructed. If DS requires demonstrable per-block
bundle hashes, a re-run under the seed daemon patched to persist
source_provenance.json would be needed (no rerun performed now).
